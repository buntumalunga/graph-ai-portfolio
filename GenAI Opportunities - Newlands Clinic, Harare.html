Executive Summary

Newlands Clinic plays a critical role in HIV care and healthcare training in Zimbabwe. Implementing Generative AI (GenAI) requires a "Human-in-the-loop" approach to ensure that patient confidentiality, data sovereignty, and clinical accuracy remain paramount.

High-Impact Clinical & Operational Use Cases

Use Case Rationale
AI-Assisted Clinical Documentation Transcribing patient consultations to reduce administrative burden, allowing staff to focus on the 8,000+ registered patients.
Educational Content Generation Creating culturally relevant, localized patient education materials regarding ART adherence in Shona and English.
Research & Literature Synthesis Summarizing large volumes of complex HIV research papers to support the clinic’s ongoing medical research initiatives.
Training Simulation Using AI agents to act as "standardized patients" for training healthcare workers in counseling and diagnosis scenarios.

Ethical Governance Framework

  • Data Privacy: Implement strictly non-identifiable, anonymized datasets for all AI processing. Avoid feeding Personal Health Information (PHI) into public LLMs.
  • Algorithmic Bias: Ensure models are tested against local demographic data to prevent biases that could impact HIV treatment outcomes.
  • Clinical Oversight: All AI-generated outputs must be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional before application in clinical decision-making.

Suggested Next Steps

  1. Policy Formulation: Establish an internal "AI Ethics Committee" at Newlands Clinic to review AI tool selection.
  2. Pilot Program: Select one low-risk administrative area (e.g., summarizing non-sensitive research literature) to validate performance.
  3. Infrastructure Audit: Assess current data storage capabilities to ensure compliance with international and Zimbabwean data protection standards.
