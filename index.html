about

I am a Clinical Research Assistant at the Newlands Clinic in Harare and a Professional Cook specialized in Culinary Nutrition. My work is situated at the intersection of biochemistry, public health, and culinary arts.

Holding a BSc Honours in Biochemistry from the University of Zimbabwe and a Culinary Nutrition Professional Diploma (Distinction) from the British Academy of Culinary Nutrition, I develop practical, evidence-based methods for sustainable dietary changes.

Currently, I analyze NCDs in HIV-positive cohorts, build R-driven workflows, and run 408°F | The Kitchen Lab, exploring the limits of flavor, modern nutrition, and health.
